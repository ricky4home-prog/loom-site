Loom Privacy Policy

Loom is a single-user personal application. It is used only by its developer, on the developer's own computer.

Data accessed

With the developer's consent, Loom reads and edits the developer's own Google Calendar events and Google Tasks.

Storage and sharing

Credentials and data stay on the developer's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with, or disclosed to any third party.
